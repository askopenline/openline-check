    eSIM questions, answered

    How do I check if my phone supports eSIM?

    Search your brand and model above, or the model code printed in your phone’s settings. You can also dial *#06#: if an EID line appears, the device has eSIM hardware.

    What is an EID?

    The EID (eUICC identifier) is a 32-digit number that identifies the eSIM chip inside your device. Only devices with eSIM hardware have one.

    Why can the same model differ by region?

    Manufacturers ship regional versions. Most iPhones sold in mainland China, Hong Kong and Macao use two physical SIMs instead of eSIM, while US iPhone 14 and later are eSIM-only. Check the exact model code.

    Does my phone need to be unlocked?

    Yes. A carrier-locked phone may refuse eSIM profiles from other providers even if it has eSIM hardware. Ask your carrier to unlock it before installing a new eSIM.

    How do I install an Openline eSIM?

    If your device is compatible and unlocked, buy a plan at openline.com and scan the QR code under Settings › Cellular (iPhone) or Settings › Connections › SIM manager (most Android phones).
