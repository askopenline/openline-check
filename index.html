    Rules of thumb

    Yes

    Every iPhone from XS / XR (2018) onward, all iPads with cellular from 2018+, and every Apple Watch Cellular — except units bought in mainland China, Hong Kong, or Macao.

    Yes

    Samsung Galaxy S20 and newer, Note 20 series, all foldables (Z Fold / Z Flip), the A54 / A55 / A56 / A57, and Galaxy Watch LTE from 3 onward.

    Yes

    Google Pixel 5 and newer, all Pixel Watch LTE, all iPads cellular, cellular Galaxy Tab S7+ and later, and modern 5G mobile hotspots (Nighthawk M5+, Solis 5G, GlocalMe).

    No

    Any phone from before 2018 (with rare exceptions), most Redmi / POCO, Samsung A/M/F series below A54, Wi-Fi-only tablets, non-cellular smartwatches, and every feature phone.
